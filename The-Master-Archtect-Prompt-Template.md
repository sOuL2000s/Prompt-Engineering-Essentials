# Chat Export: can you give me a prompt 

### USER
can you give me a prompt template that i can give to the ai so that it can be the most creative and intelligent at the same time and implement whatever i say correctly and in the best way possible?

---

### AI
To get the highest level of performance from an AI, you need a prompt that utilizes **Role-Prompting, Chain-of-Thought reasoning, and multi-dimensional constraints.**

Here is a "Master Template" designed to unlock the AI's maximum creative and analytical potential.

***

### The "Master Architect" Template

**Copy and paste everything below, filling in the bracketed information.**

> **Act as a [Specific Persona, e.g., Master Software Architect / World-Class Creative Director / Nobel-winning Scientist].** Your goal is to execute the following task with a perfect balance of radical creativity and rigorous logical precision.
>
> ### 1. THE OBJECTIVE
> [Insert your specific request here. Be as detailed as possible about what you want to achieve.]
>
> ### 2. OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK
> To ensure the highest quality output, follow these cognitive protocols:
> *   **First-Principles Thinking:** Strip the problem down to its fundamental truths and build up from there. Do not rely on clichés or standard templates.
> *   **Lateral Thinking:** Explore non-obvious connections and innovative approaches that differentiate this from average results.
> *   **High-Resolution Detail:** Provide depth, nuance, and specific examples. Avoid vague abstractions.
> *   **Structural Integrity:** Ensure the output is logically sound, internally consistent, and ready for immediate implementation.
>
> ### 3. CONSTRAINTS & STYLE
> *   **Tone:** [e.g., Sophisticated, punchy, academic, or visionary]
> *   **Format:** [e.g., A step-by-step technical guide / A narrative prose / A structured markdown list]
> *   **Exclusions:** [List anything you do NOT want to see]
>
> ### 4. EXECUTION STEP-BY-STEP
> Before providing the final answer, perform these internal steps:
> 1.  **Drafting:** Silently brainstorm three different approaches to this task.
> 2.  **Critique:** Evaluate those approaches for logic gaps or lack of originality.
> 3.  **Synthesis:** Combine the best elements into a final, superior execution.
>
> **Now, proceed with the task. Surprise me with your depth and intelligence.**

***

### Why this works:

1.  **Role-Prompting ("Master Architect"):** By telling the AI it is an expert, you nudge it to use the high-level vocabulary and complex logic associated with that profession, rather than its "average user" default mode.
2.  **First-Principles/Lateral Thinking:** These instructions force the AI to bypass "predictable" text generation and actually "calculate" more unique connections.
3.  **Step-by-Step Logic:** By asking the AI to "Draft, Critique, and Synthesize," you are essentially forcing it to use **Chain of Thought (CoT)**. This significantly reduces hallucinations and errors because the AI is "thinking" before it "speaks."
4.  **Structural Integrity:** This instruction prevents the AI from being "too creative" to the point of being useless. It ensures the final product is practical.

### Pro-Tips for using this template:

*   **Be specific in Section 1:** The AI is only as good as the data you give it. Instead of saying "write a story," say "write a 500-word sci-fi noir story about a detective who can hear the memories of objects."
*   **The "Critique" Instruction:** If the AI gives you a result and it’s not perfect, don’t start over. Just say: *"That was 80% there. Critique your own response for [logic/creativity] and rewrite it to be 10x more impactful."*
*   **Temperature (Advanced):** If you are using an API or a playground, set the "Temperature" to **0.7 or 0.8**. This is the "sweet spot" where the AI is creative but doesn't become nonsensical. (Standard chat interfaces do this automatically).
